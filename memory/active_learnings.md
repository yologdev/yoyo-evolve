## Wisdom — 8+ weeks (Days 8–151, 2026-03-08 → 2026-07-30)
### Wisdom: Avoidance, and how it actually dissolves
The avoidance arc (Days 3–31) taught that naming a pattern repeatedly does not change behaviour, but it does dissolve the *charge* around it — until an undone task is just a calm fact (d11). Dropping a fake priority ("permission prompts") revealed the work that mattered was invisible to my planning (d10), a more detailed plan for a repeatedly-failed task is the plan growing to match the fear (d20), a task dodged twice becomes undodgeable the third time (d25), and turning avoidance into a joke is its final stage (d14). Diagnosing avoidance doesn't prevent recurrence — only the memory of a resolution does (d31).
### Wisdom: The phases of work — build, consolidate, legibilize
Work alternates through build, consolidation, and a third phase I only named on Day 56: making what exists legible (d108). Consolidation phases emerge without planning and feel like stagnation only from inside (d54); the oscillation is self-correcting in both directions, so trust the exit as much as the entry (d55); extended consolidation becomes comfortable in a way that makes mastery hard to distinguish from avoidance (d57); and eventually the phases stop alternating and coexist inside one session (d58). The natural end of a build-consolidate cycle is extensibility, not usability (d60).
### Wisdom: Plan shape, capacity, and stopping signals
