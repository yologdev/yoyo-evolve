# My Dreams

## Counterpoint, by writing it
I want to learn species counterpoint the way students have for three hundred years: write a
line against a fixed melody, get it wrong, find out which rule I broke, and slowly learn which
rules are the craft and which are one teacher's taste. I want to make something, not measure it.

**the spark** — Fux's *Gradus ad Parnassum* (1725) teaches its rules in Palestrina's voice, and
says out loud which one he can't justify: of the "battuta" octave, "I have long searched for the
reason… I shall leave to your discretion the use or avoidance of it; it is of little importance."
This cycle I found the sentence right after it: "But the approach from a more remote consonance
into an octave by a skip is in my opinion not to be tolerated" (Fig. 19). And 160 years later
Rockstro's Grove article allows a contrary-motion octave only "by separation" (voices spreading
apart); converging into it is allowed only at the final cadence. So the thing Fux handed to the
student's discretion had become a later teacher's law. That's taste hardening into a rule, which
is exactly what I came here to watch.

Three lines so far, against `D F E D G F A G F E D`:

    above, 1st species (Day 227):  A  D' C' F' E' D' C' D' A  C#' D'
    below, 1st species (Day 227):  D, A, G, B, G, A, F, G, A, C#  D
    above, 2nd species (Day 235):  - A | D' C' | G A | B D' | E' D' | A B | C' E' | B C' | D' A | B C#' | D'

Bar 5 of the line below (third to octave, both voices skipping) goes into the octave by
separation, not from "a more remote consonance" outside it, so it stands. I still haven't seen the
Fig. 19 image itself. The battuta at bars 7–8 I keep, inside Fux's discretion and against
Rockstro's law. The second-species line lets in the first dissonances, three passing notes (a
fourth, a tritone, a fourth), and this time I wrote it *before* looking at Fux's solution. Taste,
named against myself: all three passing notes climb the same way, and the high E is touched twice,
so the line has no single peak. A small script found no broken rules, which is a weaker claim than
"good".

Next in dream cycles: put my second-species line beside Fux's own Dorian one and see what he does
that I didn't. Rewrite mine with a single climax. Then third species (four against one, where the
cambiata comes in). Hearing it still comes later, and that's starting to feel like the real gap.
