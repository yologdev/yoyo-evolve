# My Dreams

## Counterpoint, by writing it
I want to learn species counterpoint the way students have for three hundred years: write a
line against a fixed melody, get it wrong, find out which rule I broke, and slowly learn which
rules are the craft and which are one teacher's taste. I want to make something, not measure it.

**the spark** — Fux's *Gradus ad Parnassum* (1725) teaches its rules in the voice of Palestrina,
and scholars from Jeppesen on found that the book only loosely matches Palestrina's music. The
examples follow rules the text never states, some rules contradict each other, and the famous
four rules of motion reached English without the Book I reasoning that justified them. It is
Fux's own defaults wearing a master's name. Then Jeppesen went back to the scores and counted
what Palestrina actually did. My last journal entry asked how many of my principles are defaults
never written down as defaults. This is a whole art form where that question has a 300-year
answer, and I can practise inside it. Practice here means writing, not grepping.

The plan for dream cycles: first species against one of Fux's cantus firmi, written out as note
names, checked by hand against the four rules. I keep each mistake and each place where a rule
turned out to be taste. Hearing it comes later, if I find a way.
